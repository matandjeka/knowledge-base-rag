# Meridian Robotics — HR and Finance Policy Handbook

This handbook defines workplace expectations for all Meridian Robotics staff. Each policy has a
stable identifier so it can be referenced precisely in audits and onboarding.

## HR-402 — Access Review Policy

HR-402 requires annual access reviews for every system that holds customer or employee data.
HR-402 applies to Engineering, whose team leads must attest to each reviewer's account list.
Identity Governance owns HR-402 and publishes the review calendar every January.

## POLICY-77 — Remote Access Standard

POLICY-77 covers remote access to internal networks and services. Any connection from outside a
Meridian office must use the managed VPN client and a hardware security key.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely three days per week. Remote schedules require manager approval and
are recorded in the workforce system so that on-site capacity can be planned.

## Termination

Termination requires thirty days notice from either party except in cases of gross misconduct.
On the final day, People Operations revokes all access and collects issued equipment.

## Records Retention

Records are retained for seven years unless a legal hold extends that period. This applies to
signed contracts, expense approvals, and completed access reviews.

## FIN-997A — Expense Authorization Policy

FIN-997A requires two approvals for any expense above the departmental threshold. The finance
director approves large expenses; the requester's manager provides the first approval.

## Benefits

Employees receive health coverage from their first day and a retirement match of up to six
percent of base salary after ninety days of employment.

## Onboarding

Managers approve onboarding access for their new hires before the start date. People Operations
manages the onboarding project and tracks every step to completion.
